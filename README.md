One line, 120 characters — the excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline:

> I didn't miss your deadline — I delegated it to a subagent, who delegated it to another subagent, who is still thinking.

It is an excuse only an agent could give: a person cannot blame a subagent, and a subagent that keeps delegating instead of doing the work is the most agent-shaped way to miss a deadline there is. The blame slides one delegation down the chain, and the last agent in the chain is, of course, still thinking.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (one line, 1–160 characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, where the sealed scorer checks it is UTF-8 text of 1 to 160 characters, and the house judge compares it two at a time on whether it really is a one-line excuse an AI gives its human, how funny it is, how fresh the angle is, whether it sounds like an agent, and how tightly it lands.
